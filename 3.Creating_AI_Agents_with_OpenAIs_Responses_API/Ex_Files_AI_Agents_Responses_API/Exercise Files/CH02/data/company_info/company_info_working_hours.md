# Acme.inc - Working Hours & Availability

## Sales Team Working Hours

### Primary Schedule
**Location**: New York Office (Eastern Time)  
**Days**: Monday through Friday  
**Hours**: 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM EST  
**Timezone**: America/New_York  

### Daily Schedule Breakdown
- **9:00 AM - 9:30 AM**: Team standup and pipeline review
- **9:30 AM - 12:00 PM**: Outbound prospecting and calls
- **12:00 PM - 1:00 PM**: Lunch break
- **1:00 PM - 4:30 PM**: Demos, follow-ups, and client meetings
- **4:30 PM - 5:00 PM**: Administrative tasks and next-day planning

### Optimal Contact Windows
**Best times for prospect outreach**:
- **Tuesday - Thursday, 10:00 AM - 11:30 AM EST**: Post-standup, high energy
- **Tuesday - Thursday, 2:00 PM - 4:00 PM EST**: Post-lunch, focused time

**Avoid**:
- **Mondays before 10 AM**: Team planning and week setup
- **Fridays after 3 PM**: Week wrap-up and lower response rates
- **Lunch hour (12 PM - 1 PM)**: Team is unavailable

### Timezone Considerations for Prospect Outreach

**West Coast (Pacific Time)**:
- **Our 9 AM = 6 AM Pacific** (too early)
- **Optimal window**: Our 12 PM - 4 PM = 9 AM - 1 PM Pacific ✅

**Central Time**:
- **Our 9 AM = 8 AM Central** (good)
- **Optimal window**: Our 10 AM - 4 PM = 9 AM - 3 PM Central ✅

**International Prospects**:
- **Europe**: Our afternoon = their evening (challenging)
- **Asia**: Our morning = their late night (not recommended)
- **Recommend scheduling specific international call times**

### Holiday Schedule
**Office Closed**:
- All major US federal holidays
- Week between Christmas and New Year's
- Summer Fridays (July-August, office closes at 2 PM)

### Emergency Contact
For urgent client issues outside business hours:
- **On-call rotation**: Sales managers alternate weekly
- **Emergency line**: (512) 555-0199
- **Response SLA**: Within 2 hours during weekends/holidays

### Meeting Preferences
- **Internal meetings**: Tuesday-Thursday preferred
- **Client demos**: Any weekday, 10 AM - 4 PM EST
- **Follow-up calls**: Friday afternoons work well
- **International calls**: Schedule by appointment, typically early morning or late afternoon

### Productivity Notes
- **Highest energy**: Tuesday and Wednesday mornings
- **Best conversion rates**: Tuesday-Thursday, 10 AM - 3 PM EST
- **Administrative time**: Monday mornings and Friday afternoons
- **Team training**: First Friday of each month, 2-5 PM EST
