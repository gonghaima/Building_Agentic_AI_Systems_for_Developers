# Acme.inc - Company Profile

## Company Overview
**Founded**: 2019  
**Headquarters**: Austin, Texas  
**Employees**: 85 people  
**Mission**: "Making work life deliciously better, one snack at a time"

## Leadership Team
- **CEO**: Jessica Martinez (Former VP at Whole Foods, Stanford MBA)
- **CTO**: David Chen (Ex-Google, built recommendation systems)
- **VP Sales**: Marcus Johnson (Previously at Slack, grew revenue 10x)

## Company Culture
- **Remote-first** with Austin HQ for local team gatherings
- **Customer obsessed** - we read every piece of feedback
- **Data-driven** but human-centered decision making
- **Work-life balance** - we practice what we preach about happiness

## Business Model
- **B2B SaaS** with physical product fulfillment
- **Recurring revenue** from monthly subscriptions
- **95% gross margins** on software, 25% on snack fulfillment
- **$2.1M ARR** as of Q4 2024, growing 40% YoY

## Recent Milestones
- **Series A**: $8M led by Bessemer Venture Partners (Q3 2024)
- **Customer Growth**: 150+ companies, 8,000+ happy snackers
- **Product Launch**: AI-powered mood tracking (Q4 2024)
- **Partnership**: Exclusive deal with premium snack supplier network
