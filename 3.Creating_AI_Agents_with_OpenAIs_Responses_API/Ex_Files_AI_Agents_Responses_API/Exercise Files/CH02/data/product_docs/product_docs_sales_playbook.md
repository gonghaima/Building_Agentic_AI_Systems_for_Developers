# Sales Playbook - SnackBot Pro

## Qualifying Prospects

### Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
- **Company Size**: 15-500 employees
- **Budget**: $100-400/month for employee perks
- **Pain Points**: Low team morale, high turnover, empty snack cabinets
- **Values**: Employee happiness, company culture, wellness initiatives
- **Decision Makers**: HR Manager, Office Manager, CEO (smaller companies)

### Discovery Questions
1. "How do you currently handle office snacks and beverages?"
2. "What's your biggest challenge with keeping the team happy and energized?"
3. "How often do you run out of snacks or have disappointed employees?"
4. "What's your monthly spend on team perks and office supplies?"
5. "How important is employee happiness to your company culture?"

## Value Propositions by Company Type

### Tech Startups
- **Primary Pain**: Keeping developers happy during long coding sessions
- **Our Solution**: Smart caffeine + healthy snack delivery based on energy levels
- **ROI Pitch**: "Reduce developer burnout, increase retention by 40%"
- **Case Study**: TechFlow Solutions saw 50% fewer 'hangry' Slack messages

### Creative Agencies
- **Primary Pain**: Fuel needed for late-night brainstorming and client deadlines
- **Our Solution**: Mood-based snack delivery that sparks creativity
- **ROI Pitch**: "Keep creative energy flowing, reduce project delays"
- **Creative Hook**: "Feed inspiration, not just hunger"

### Co-working Spaces
- **Primary Pain**: Attracting and retaining members in competitive market
- **Our Solution**: Premium snack experience as a membership differentiator
- **ROI Pitch**: "Increase member satisfaction, reduce churn by 30%"
- **Revenue Hook**: Additional revenue stream through member upgrades

### Small Offices
- **Primary Pain**: Office manager spending too much time on snack runs
- **Our Solution**: Completely automated snack management
- **ROI Pitch**: "Save 5 hours/week of admin time, happier employees"
- **Simplicity Hook**: "Set it and forget it - we handle everything"

## Objection Handling

### "We already buy snacks from Costco"
**Response**: "That's smart! How often do you find yourself making emergency runs when you're out? SnackBot Pro takes that completely off your plate and learns what your team actually eats vs. what sits there forever."

### "Sounds expensive for just snacks"
**Response**: "I get it! But think about it this way - you're already spending money on snacks, plus someone's time to buy and manage them. We typically save companies money while making everyone happier."

### "Our team has dietary restrictions"
**Response**: "Perfect! That's exactly why we built SnackBot Pro. We track individual preferences - gluten-free, vegan, keto, allergies - everything. No more accidentally buying snacks half your team can't eat."

### "Need to check with the team first"
**Response**: "Absolutely! Actually, would it help if we sent a sample snack box this week? Your team can try it out and you'll see their reaction firsthand. No commitment required."

## Demo Script

### Opening (5 minutes)
1. Confirm current snack situation from discovery call
2. Set agenda: "I'll show you how SnackBot Pro would work in your office"
3. Ask: "What would the perfect snack experience look like for your team?"

### Current State (10 minutes)
1. Show typical office snack problems (empty shelves, stale snacks, waste)
2. Calculate hidden costs of current approach
3. Identify team preferences and dietary needs

### SnackBot Demo (15 minutes)
1. Walk through the smart dashboard and preference learning
2. Show how automatic reordering works
3. Demonstrate mood-based snack suggestions

### Fun Features (10 minutes)
1. Show "snack stats" and team challenges
2. Demonstrate surprise delivery features
3. Show integration with Slack for mood tracking

### Pricing & Next Steps (10 minutes)
1. Show ROI compared to current spending
2. Offer trial snack box program
3. Schedule setup call or pilot program

## Pricing Strategy

### Value-Based Pricing
Focus on happiness ROI rather than just cost:
- "What's employee retention worth to you?"
- "How much do you spend on recruiting when people leave for better perks?"
- "Happy employees are 13% more productive - what's that worth?"

### Pilot Programs
For all prospects, offer risk-free trial:
- Send sample snack box for one week
- No setup fees, no commitment
- "Let your team's reaction sell itself"

### Budget Positioning
Start with Deluxe ($199) as the "recommended" option, making Starter ($99) feel like a steal and Enterprise ($399) feel comprehensive.

## Competitive Positioning

### vs. Traditional Office Supply Companies
- **Their Strength**: Established relationships, bulk pricing
- **Our Advantage**: Personalized, automated, fun experience
- **Messaging**: "Office Depot feeds the office, we feed the team"

### vs. Manual Snack Management
- **Their Strength**: Full control, familiar process
- **Our Advantage**: Saves time, prevents stockouts, learns preferences
- **Messaging**: "You wouldn't manually send emails - why manually manage snacks?"

### vs. Other Snack Services
- **Their Strength**: Some automation, variety
- **Our Advantage**: AI-powered personalization, mood integration, team features
- **Messaging**: "They deliver snacks, we deliver happiness"
