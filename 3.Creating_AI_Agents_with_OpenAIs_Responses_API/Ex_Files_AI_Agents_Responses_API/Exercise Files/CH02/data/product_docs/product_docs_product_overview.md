# Acme.inc - SnackBot Pro

## Product Overview
SnackBot Pro is Acme.inc's delightfully smart office snack management system that keeps your team happy, healthy, and productive. Think of it as having a friendly snack fairy that knows exactly what everyone wants before they even know it themselves!

## Key Features

### 🍪 Smart Snack Prediction
- Learns individual team members' snack preferences
- Predicts cravings based on weather, time of day, and stress levels
- Suggests healthy alternatives during "sugar crash" hours

### 📦 Auto-Restocking Magic
- Monitors snack levels with smart shelf sensors
- Automatically orders refills before you run out
- Negotiates bulk discounts with local suppliers

### 🎯 Team Mood Optimization
- Tracks team energy levels through Slack integration
- Deploys "emergency chocolate" during crunch time
- Celebrates milestones with surprise treat deliveries

### 📊 Wellness Dashboard
- Fun nutrition insights without being preachy
- Gamified healthy eating challenges
- "Snack stats" that make everyone smile

## Target Markets
- **Tech Startups**: Keep developers caffeinated and happy
- **Creative Agencies**: Fuel those late-night brainstorming sessions  
- **Co-working Spaces**: Attract and retain members with amazing snacks
